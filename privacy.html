1. Introduction

Welcome to FreshAI ("we," "our," or "us"). FreshAI is a food expiry tracking and AI-powered recipe suggestion app available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it. Please read it carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

When you use FreshAI, you may provide us with:

  • Food item names, quantities, and expiry dates you add to your inventory
  • Household size and dietary preferences set during onboarding
  • No account is required. Your identity is managed through your iCloud account
  • Device preferences and settings you configure within the app

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use FreshAI, we may automatically collect:

  • App usage data (features used, screens visited, session duration)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data to improve app stability
  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Anonymous analytics to understand how users interact with the app

2.3 Camera and photo access

FreshAI requests access to your device camera solely to scan grocery receipts and food item labels. We do not store photos taken during scanning. Images are processed in real time and immediately discarded after the relevant text is extracted.

3. AI Processing and Third-Party AI Services

FreshAI uses artificial intelligence to power two core features: food item recognition from scanned receipts, and personalized recipe suggestions based on your expiring ingredients.

To provide these features, FreshAI sends data to Anthropic, PBC, the maker of the Claude AI. Specifically:

  • Text extracted from scanned receipts is sent to Anthropic's API to identify food items and estimate expiry dates
  • Your list of expiring ingredients is sent to Anthropic's API to generate recipe suggestions

Data sent to Anthropic is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy, available at anthropic.com/privacy. We do not send personally identifiable information (such as your name or email) to Anthropic as part of these requests. Anthropic may retain API request data in accordance with their own data retention policies.

We minimize data sent to third-party AI services and only transmit the minimum information necessary to fulfill your request.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the FreshAI app and its features
  • Generate AI-powered recipe suggestions based on your fridge contents
  • Send you push notifications about expiring food items
  • Sync your data across your devices via iCloud
  • Improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • Analyze app usage to improve performance and features
  • Respond to your support requests and communicate with you
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

5. iCloud and Data Sync

FreshAI users benefit from iCloud sync, which keeps your fridge inventory consistent across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your data synced via iCloud is stored in your personal iCloud account and is subject to Apple's iCloud Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud-stored data.

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Anthropic, PBC, solely to process AI feature requests as described in Section 3
  • With Apple, Inc., for App Store transactions, iCloud sync, and push notifications
  • With analytics providers (such as anonymized crash reporting tools) to improve app stability
  • If required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, in which case we will notify you before your data is transferred

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as you use the app or as needed to provide you with its services. If you request deletion of your data, we will delete or anonymize your information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

Food inventory data stored locally on your device is fully under your control and can be deleted at any time from within the app or by deleting the app itself.

8. Children's Privacy

FreshAI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below.

9. Your Privacy Rights

California residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of your personal information, the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

European residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights including: the right to access your personal data, the right to correct inaccurate data, the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"), the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, and the right to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 12.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your fridge data is stored locally on your device and synced via your personal iCloud account. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
